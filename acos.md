AC OS 1.0 Infdev is an experimental hybrid operating system from Annoying Cat Holdings that combines Unix command-line capabilities with early Windows-style interface elements. Each boot initiates a new Infdev cycle, connecting to dream.azure.cloud through flamescoworks://portal/0.1.1 to synchronize experimental services, updates, and AI system components. The design prioritizes continuous evolution on startup, full internal visibility, rapid iteration, and kernel-level control by the cat.
